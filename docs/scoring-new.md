# NFL game watchability scoring

Evaluate completed games only. Add points from every applicable category. Within
each category, award only the highest applicable value.

## Scoring criteria

| Category | Condition | Points |
|---|---|---:|
| General scoring | Combined final score is more than 28 | +1 |
| Close finish | Final margin is 3–8 points | +1 |
| | Final margin is 0–2 points | +2 |
| Overtime | Game reached overtime, including a tied result | +1 |
| Comeback | A team erased a deficit of at least 15 points during the second half | +1 |
| | A team erased a deficit of at least 8 points during Q4 | +2 |
| Late drama | A tying or go-ahead score occurred during Q4, or a team trailing by 1–8 points had possession with three minutes or less remaining in Q4 | +1 |
| | A tying or go-ahead score occurred with five minutes or less remaining in Q4 | +2 |
| Lead changes | The lead changed 3–4 times | +1 |
| | The lead changed at least 5 times | +2 |
| Shootout | Combined final score is at least 55, and either the final margin was at most 10 or the margin was at most 7 at any time during Q4 | +1 |
| | Combined final score is at least 65, and the margin was at most 10 at the end of Q3, at any time during Q4, or at the end of the game | +2 |
| Team quality | Both teams entered with at least two more wins than losses each | +1 |
| Playoff significance | Both teams entered with at least seven more wins than losses each | +1 |

The maximum total is 14 points. There is no losing-record penalty.

## Definitions and counting rules

- **Margin:** The absolute difference between the teams' scores. End-of-Q3
  margin uses cumulative scores through three quarters.
- **Combined final score:** The sum of both teams' final scores, including
  overtime. General scoring requires **29+** points; the higher shootout tier
  requires **65+** points.
- **Comeback:** The trailing team subsequently ties the game or takes the lead;
  it does not need to win. Deficits already present at halftime or the start of
  Q4 count. Recovery must occur during the second half for the +1 tier, or during
  Q4 for the +2 tier.
- **Lead change:** A switch between the teams that lead. A → tie → B counts
  once; A → tie → A counts zero. The first team to lead does not count.
- **Late drama:** Later scoring, including an extra point, does not cancel an
  earned bonus. The late-chance condition includes exactly 3:00 remaining in
  Q4 and requires the trailing team to have possession while down by 1–8 points;
  it does not need to score or win. Award the highest applicable tier once per
  game, capped at +2 even if both a late chance and a qualifying score occur.
- **Shootout competitiveness:** A qualifying margin at any point during Q4 is
  sufficient, regardless of the final margin. Include the score carried into
  Q4 as well as subsequent score states. The combined-score threshold applies
  to the final score; it does not need to have been reached when the qualifying
  margin occurred. Award at most +2 for the shootout category.
- **Pregame record differential:** Wins minus losses before kickoff. Ties do not
  affect this calculation. Use this differential for both team quality and
  playoff significance.
- **Playoff significance:** A record-based indicator of a potential postseason
  matchup, rather than a check of actual playoff qualification. Both teams must
  individually have a pregame record differential of at least +7.
- **Stacking:** General scoring and shootout points stack, so a qualifying
  65+ point shootout earns +3 across those categories. Team quality and playoff
  significance also stack: if both teams have a pregame record differential of
  at least +7, award +2 across those categories.
- **Low-scoring games:** The general-scoring bonus is a preference, not an
  exclusion rule. A game with 28 or fewer combined points can still qualify
  through other criteria.

## Recommendations and stars

| Total | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Below 3 | Not recommended |
| 3–5 | Recommended |
| 6+ | Recommended with ⭐ |
| Highest total among recommended games | ⭐⭐⭐, replacing any single star; award to every game tied for the highest total |

If no game reaches 3 points, do not recommend or award stars to any game.
